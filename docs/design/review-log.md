# Design review log

Five critics reviewed the architect's draft of [`design.md`](design.md) on 2026-11-13: fs-safety, iced, scan-perf, ux and implementability. Before deciding, each finding was checked against the draft and the research. These claims were checked directly in the sources:
- the `trash` 5.2.9 freedesktop fallback (`freedesktop.rs` 36-66, 583-648) and the text-only macOS error (`macos/mod.rs` 100-104);
- the scan-bench termination protocol (`queue.rs` 172-183, `scanner.rs` 300-338, 554);
- the catalog's `mac-system` NoHeuristics rule on `/private` (`catalog.rs` 1126-1133, `engine.rs` 533) together with `$TMPDIR=/var/folders/…`;
- the Protect file rule `win-system-files`;
- the probe's `needs_ticks`;
- scan-bench `tests.rs`'s dependency on `TreeModel`;
- the installed rust targets.

The iced and winit source citations were consistent with API.md and the probe, so they were accepted.

No finding was rejected. Where a fix differs from the critic's recommendation, the row says "partly accepted" and explains why. Duplicate findings from different lenses share one fix.

Totals: 64 findings (2 blockers, 29 major, 33 minor). 57 accepted, 7 partly accepted (#2, #9, #13, #20, #31, #36, #44), 0 rejected.
